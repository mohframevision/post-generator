Post Maker العربية

Why this tool?

Most small business owners need one simple Instagram post: a sale, an opening, the hours, an announcement. Design apps hand you thousands of templates and drag-and-drop tools, and you end up spending your time choosing instead of posting. Here there is not a single design decision to make: you type, and you pick.

How do I use it?

Choose a post type and a size (square for a normal post, or story), then type your text and your business name. Eight different designs appear. If you don't like them, hit "More designs" — and if you clicked too fast, "Back" returns the exact same set. When one works for you, hit Download or Share.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes, and that is what it was built for first. Type in Arabic and the tool detects it automatically: it switches to real Arabic typefaces and flips the layout right-to-left. Arabic letters join correctly, which most design tools and every AI image generator still get wrong. There is a full Arabic version of this page too.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes. Pick an image from your device and it becomes the background of every design, with a light wash over it so your text stays readable whether the photo is bright or dark. It is cropped to fit automatically — no editing needed.

Can I lock in my brand colours?

Yes, under "Your brand". Choose two colours and a font, upload your logo, and every design follows your identity. Your settings are saved in your browser, so you only set them once.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything runs inside your browser — your text and photos are never uploaded anywhere, no account is needed, and even the fonts are served from this site, so no third party learns about your visit.
